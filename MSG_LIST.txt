Seat engine: a party with no list cannot take a list seat

PR seats are filled from a submitted list. A party that contested wards
only has nothing to fill them from, so it can hold no more seats than the
wards it won. The engine was handing them list seats anyway, which pushed
out the parties that should have had them.

No list is inferred from zero votes on the PR ballot - the only signal the
published results carry. The evidence is unambiguous: 147 party rows in the
2026 results have zero PR votes and NOT ONE was awarded a PR seat.

This is a plain bug rather than a judgement call, so it is default
behaviour. It improved every quota configuration and broke neither 2021
fixture, which is what a real correctness fix looks like.

The same cap then had to be applied in the OVERHANG branch, which
recomputes against a new quota and handed the seats straight back. The
three councils still wrong at that point were all in that branch. A rule
enforced in one path and not the other is not enforced.

Against 171 declared councils, with the harness faults also fixed:

  droop + independents' votes in A   109 of 171  64%   <- shipped default
  droop + votes out                  105 of 171  61%
  hare  + votes in                   152 of 171  89%
  hare  + votes out                  169 of 171  98.8%

The 2021 fixtures pass on the shipped default and FAIL on hare+out. Both
ends cannot be right. Most likely Schedule 1 changed between the two
elections - Act 3 of 2021 amended it, and both differences concern exactly
how independents and the quota interact - but that is a hypothesis from the
data, not from the statute, and nobody has read the statute yet.

Defaults therefore stay where they are. A 98.8% fit is persuasive enough to
be dangerous: flipping on the strength of a score is fitting the rule to the
data. Compare with:
  npm run seats:verify-2026 -- --quota hare --ind-votes out

Two councils still differ (EC153, LIM344), both in the overhang path.

Seat projections remain unvalidated until the quota question is settled.
