Load the IEC's published 2026 results; the seat engine fails 41% of them

The IEC publishes its results dashboard as static JSON, with no login. It
goes municipality -> ward -> voting district. The credentials this project
waited on were never needed for any of it.

This also corrects an earlier finding here. The note recorded while chasing
a 2021 baseline said public IEC reports are municipality-level only. That
was true of the detailed REPORTS and false as a general claim - the
dashboard's own data files go all the way down.

TWO THINGS LOADED

1. Council sizes. council_seats.total_seats was NULL for all 213 councils,
   so the seat engine refused to project any of them - correctly, since
   s22(2) makes a council 2w or 2w-1 seats and the difference moves the
   quota. The IEC publishes AvailableSeats for every council. All 213 now
   have a confirmed total, and ZERO conflicted with what our ward register
   implies, which cross-validates the register as a side effect.

2. Per-party results for all 213 councils (4,287 rows), in their own table.
   iec_municipality_result is deliberately NOT joinable with result_vote:
   if IEC figures could reach result_vote then v_tally_vote would include
   them and a tally commitment would seal numbers copied from the IEC as
   though we had captured them - the exact claim the commitment module
   exists to make checkable.

THE FINDING

seats:verify-2026 runs the engine against every council the IEC reports
complete. It reproduces 100 of 175.

The two 2021 fixtures it passed were not wrong, they were unrepresentative
- both were picked because their results were published conveniently, which
is the selection bias that makes a small fixture set reassuring instead of
informative.

What is established so far:
- The model shape is right. For 154 of 175, some single integer quota plus
  largest-remainder top-up reproduces the IEC's entitlements exactly, so
  compensatory allocation is confirmed - independently visible in the IEC's
  own mid-count rows, where the total entitlement runs ahead of the settled
  ward+PR allocation.
- The quota is wrong. The implied denominator sits consistently above
  total_seats + 1.
- 21 councils fit no single quota and need a further mechanism.

Seat projections should be treated as unvalidated until this is resolved
against the statute. seats:verify-2026 is now the real test; the 175 are the
fixture set and no more hand-picked ones should be added.
