Replace the MK-derived palette with a party-neutral one

The scheme was signal yellow #FFD600 and status green #00E676, sampled
from the MK Party logo on 23 October and carried forward under the Xnith
name. An instrument that publishes tally commitments cannot wear one
party's colours: the argument of the commitment chain is that the numbers
can be checked by someone who does not trust the people presenting them,
and a reader who recognises the livery discounts the evidence before
reading it.

VIOLET IS THE IDENTITY because it is the one vivid hue no significant
South African party claims - green reads ANC or MK, blue DA, red EFF,
orange VF Plus, and gold is where this started. Neutrality here is a
question of what a colour is not already taken to mean.

The --mk-gold and --mk-green tokens are DELETED rather than re-pointed, so
nothing can quietly resolve back to them. Every use was then repointed by
meaning rather than by hue: a live state to --st-good, an incomplete bar
to --st-warn, an active toggle to --ours. Left unrepointed they would have
resolved to nothing and those rules would have died silently, which is a
failure this stylesheet has already had once.

Status colours stay green/amber/red. That is UI semantics, not livery -
green means staffed, not a party - and they are deliberately brighter and
more saturated than any series colour so a status cannot read as a
category.

EVERY VALUE WAS MEASURED, NOT PICKED. Generated in OKLCH and run through
the dataviz validator: lightness band, chroma floor, CVD separation under
protan/deutan/tritan simulation, normal-vision floor, and contrast against
the real surface. The categorical sets clear every check on ALL pairs, not
just adjacent ones, because a hemicycle shows every party at once. Worst
case ΔE 9.3 CVD and 17.4 normal on dark, 9.6 and 15.8 on light, all six at
or above 3:1.

SIX CATEGORICAL SLOTS, NOT EIGHT. Eight vivid hues could not clear CVD
separation at any lightness assignment - simulation collapses the
red-green axis and the extra hues land on each other. A seventh party
folds into the overflow grey, which is honest; inventing two more hues
that look distinct to us and merge for one reader in twelve is not.

PARTIES NO LONGER TAKE STATUS COLOURS. SEAT_PALETTE was
['--ours','--st-info','--st-good','--st-warn','--st-bad', ...], so the
fifth-largest party rendered in alarm red and the fourth in warning amber
- colours the rest of the dashboard uses to mean something is wrong. It
now uses --cat-1..6.

The sequential ramp is re-derived from the violet and re-validated as an
ordinal scale. Its ink crossover moved from step 4 to step 3 and now lands
on the same step in both themes, so the light theme's separate s4/s5
exception is gone - with this ramp those steps are dark violet and needed
light ink, the opposite of what that rule did. Worst case 4.86:1 dark,
5.29:1 light.

Favicon, lockup SVGs and the mask-icon colour follow.

Checked in both themes across all 14 views: every token resolves, no
--mk-* remains, no console errors.
