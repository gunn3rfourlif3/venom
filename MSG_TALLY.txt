Tally commitments, and fix vote totals being counted twice

Seals each tally snapshot behind a published SHA-256 digest so a figure
shown at 20:15 can be proved to be the figure still being claimed at 23:00.

The original ask was to encrypt votes so live numbers could be shown
without revealing the party. That does not work: the IEC posts each
station's result at the station, so ground truth is public within hours
and any anonymous series joins straight back to it - and nine unlabelled
numbers from a known party list identify themselves by magnitude anyway.
Encryption hides a label; it cannot hide the shape, and with results the
shape is the label. Inverted instead: the numbers are made more checkable,
not less.

Found while building it: every vote total was DOUBLE. Each query joined
result_vote to result_transcription and summed, but a slip carries two
transcriptions - double entry is the quality mechanism - so every verified
slip counted twice. A 500-vote slip returned 1,000. The overcount drifted
between 1x and 2x as transcription caught up, which looks like results
arriving rather than a bug, and it would have doubled every seat
projection on the night.

- 053  append-only tally_commitment hash chain; triggers refuse UPDATE and
       DELETE, and prove they refuse before the migration completes
- 054  v_tally_vote / v_slip_counted / v_tally_coverage - one counted
       transcription per slip, DISPUTED slips excluded rather than guessed.
       Fails if the naive join stops doubling a known fixture
- 055  corrects 053: sealed means unpublished, not unstored. Counts are
       written at seal time and masked by the public view until revealed
- 056  records which digest was sent outside the campaign and where. A
       POINTER so the right message can be found again at 02:00 - never
       proof, since anyone who could rebuild the chain could rewrite it
- src/tally/commit.ts  canonical plain-text form and digest, pure and
       checkable by hand with sha256sum (pgcrypto is not installed)
- tally:commit / tally:reveal / tally:verify / tally:export
- GET /api/commitments and a Tally integrity screen
- seats.ts now reads v_tally_vote; verify-data gains an UNTESTED outcome so
  checks with no subject stop counting themselves as passes

Procedure is hourly seals with an anchor at 20:00, 23:00 and declaration -
each digest contains the previous one, so three messages cover eleven
seals. --anchor-now marks those cuts; it changes only what is printed, and
an ordinary seal says plainly that nothing needs sending. The old
unconditional "PUBLISH THIS LINE NOW" would have been wrong eight times
out of eleven, and an instruction that is usually wrong is one people learn
to scroll past at exactly the moment it matters.

A chain held only by us proves nothing against us - a rebuilt chain
verifies perfectly, and that is tested, including with the anchor record
doctored to match. The real check is tally:verify --anchor against the copy
in the recipient's hands. docs/TALLY-INTEGRITY.md says so plainly rather
than overselling the digest.
