Reports normally arrive by WhatsApp straight into the database. This is the manual path for when they do not — paste the batch, check what it read, then file. Nothing is written until you have seen the parse.

Who may report

—

Automated ingest accepts a message only from a number on this list. A report from anywhere else is recorded and refused, never filed — which is what stops a stranger who learns the number from putting a staffed booth on the exception list. Paste one marshal per line as Name, 082 123 4567.
