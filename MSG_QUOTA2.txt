Fix the seat quota formula; the conflicting evidence was a circular fixture

The engine divided by (seats - independents + 1) and counted independents'
ward votes in the numerator. The IEC's own voter-education material states
neither: the quota is the valid votes FOR PARTIES divided by the seats left
after independents, THEN plus one, disregarding fractions. The "+1" lands
on the quota, not on the denominator, and conflating the two is exactly
what the engine did.

Against 171 declared 2026 councils:

  seats+1 denominator, independents' votes in   109 of 171   64%   (old)
  seats denominator, votes in                   152 of 171   89%
  IEC formula, votes out                        169 of 171   98.8% (new)

WHY THIS LOOKED LIKE A RULE CHANGE

Switching to the correct rule made the 2021 fixtures fail, so the obvious
reading was that Act 3 of 2021 changed the formula between the elections -
and on that basis the defaults were left alone.

It was not a rule change. The fixtures carried expectedQuota 1865 for Sol
Plaatje and 2153 for Madibeng, and NEITHER WAS EVER PUBLISHED. The
Wikipedia pages they come from state no quota at all. Both numbers were
this engine's own output, written into the fixture as though they were
facts about the election, so the test was checking the engine against its
own assumption.

Under the correct rule both fixtures reproduce their published SEATS
exactly, with a quota three higher. The only thing that ever failed was the
self-derived assertion - and because it failed loudly, it argued for the
bug. A circular test is worse than no test.

expectedQuota is now unset in both fixtures with the reason recorded.
Seats are published; quotas are not.

- defaults are now quotaRule 'iec' and independentVotesInQuota false
- old behaviour reproducible: seats:verify-2026 --quota droop --ind-votes in
- seats-verify-2026 no longer hardcodes its own copy of the defaults, which
  would have kept it reporting on the old engine after this change

Two councils still differ, EC153 and LIM344, both in the overhang path -
the one branch still never checked against a published result.
