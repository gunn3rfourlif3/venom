Fix two seat-engine harness faults; surface an unresolved quota question

Following the 2026 baseline, three separate faults, in order of size.

1. The harness passed independents in as an ordinary party. The dashboard
   carries an aggregate pseudo-party named exactly INDEPENDENTS: ward-only,
   can never take a list seat, but its ward votes belong in the quota's
   numerator and its ward seats come out of the denominator. allocate()
   already took both as separate inputs and the harness did not use them,
   so the engine awarded list seats to "independents" and pushed real
   parties out.

   The name is matched EXACTLY. Dozens of registered parties contain
   INDEPENDENT, and THE INDEPENDENTS is a real party with 7,117 list votes
   and four PR seats; a substring test deletes them from the allocation.

2. "All districts counted" is not "allocation finished". EC122, BUF, LIM334
   and FS194 report every district in while their PR allocation has not run
   - each declares only ward seats, summing to half the council. Scoring
   those manufactured failures. A council is testable only once its
   declared seats fill it.

3. THE QUOTA DIVISOR IS UNRESOLVED. With the above fixed, against ~170
   declared councils:

     droop  A/(seats+1)   106 of 171 (62%)   2021 fixtures pass
     hare   A/seats       151 of 171 (88%)   2021 fixtures FAIL

   Both cannot be right. Either Schedule 1's quota changed between the two
   elections - Act 3 of 2021 amended it - or one fixture set is wrong. It
   moves the last seat in about a quarter of councils.

   The default STAYS on droop: the shipped behaviour, and the one that
   reproduces the two results known to be correct. Switching on the
   strength of a better score would be fitting the rule to the data.
   quotaRule is now an explicit option and seats:verify-2026 takes
   --quota hare so the comparison can be run rather than argued.

Also retracts an earlier claim of mine, in GOTCHAS: that the implied quota
denominator sat above seats+1. That was an artifact of a search returning
the smallest fitting quota, biased low by construction, and it pointed away
from the answer.

Seat projections remain UNVALIDATED until the quota is settled.
